Privacy policy

Damage Control Drive is used only by its owner, Dom Coppinger, with his own Google account.

  • What it accesses: only the Google Drive files and folders it creates itself (the drive.file permission). It can't see the rest of the Drive.
  • What it does with them: it uploads backups and game recordings into the owner's own Drive, and reads them back to check them.
  • Storage: the files stay in the owner's Google Drive. The sign-in is kept only on the owner's own computer.
  • Sharing: nothing is collected, sold or shared with anyone. There are no analytics, ads or other users.
